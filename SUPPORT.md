# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/open-rms/rigctlweb/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/open-rms/rigctlweb/issues/new/choose) with the bug report form. Include versions, your radio and Hamlib model number, and logs.
- **Radio-specific problems:** use the radio support form and attach `\dump_caps` output.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not use public channels. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Problems that come from Hamlib itself, such as a radio model that rigctld cannot talk to, are best reported to the [Hamlib project](https://github.com/Hamlib/Hamlib).

rigctlWeb is free and open source. If you would like to support it, see [docs/DONATING.md](docs/DONATING.md). Donations are optional and never required for support.
