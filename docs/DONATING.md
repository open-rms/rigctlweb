# Free as in ham radio

This project is free and open source, and it always will be. There are no license keys, no registration fees, no "pro" tier, and no features held back for people who pay.

It is built on open protocols and documented interfaces so that other software and other modems can work alongside it, and I'm glad when they do. Other projects in this space are welcome here: compatibility beats competition. If you want to integrate with it, or port it or your own software to another operating system, go ahead. I will not ship time bombs, kill switches, or deliberate interruptions to break other software, and I will not use licensing or legal threats to shut down projects that compete with mine or simply want to interoperate.

Amateur radio is a hobby of sharing and experimenting, and software for it shouldn't be a toll booth.

## Tips are welcome, never required

If this saves you time or helps your station, a tip is appreciated. A one-time donation or a small recurring one helps cover test radios, hosting, and the hours that go into it.

Donating doesn't buy support priority, extra features, or a vote on the roadmap, and not donating costs you nothing.
